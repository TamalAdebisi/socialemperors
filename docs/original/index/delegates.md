# Item behaviours (delegates)

A delegate is a small class the Flash client attaches to one unit or building, chosen by the item's id when it is placed (`DelegatesManager`). It hooks into the fight: what the unit does on each attack beat, as it walks onto a tile, when it is hit or deals damage, which shot it fires and how big it is drawn. Nearly all of them are about battles: special moves, auras, the LIMIT bar, bosses; only a handful touch buildings, and none touch the town builder's economy. In the new client this part lives in `client/src/rules/battle.ts` (plain fighting, shots, towers), `rules/specials.ts` with `rules/specials-table.json` (moves on a clock, buttons, LIMIT) and `rules/bossMinds.ts`; the special units' buttons themselves are listed in the unit classes (`core/isoengine/isounits/`), another part of this map.

**Counts:** 125 built, 215 partly, 6 not yet, 1 server, 1 not needed, 2 art (350 classes).

How to read the statuses here:

- **built** means what the class does is in the new client, give or take a picture.
- **partly** is used a lot, mostly for two gaps that recur:
  - *drawn as an arrow*: the class picks the unit's shot (a ball, a beam, a spear). Ours draws an arrow for every unit missing from the `UNIT_SHOTS` table in `rules/battle.ts`. That's the look (and the shot's timing), not the damage.
  - *hits by hand*: the class marks the unit as fighting hand to hand although its reach is 2 or more, so the hit lands at once with no shot. Ours shoots, except for the four units in `RANGED_MELEE`.
- **not yet** mostly means a unit whose moves come every so many attack beats, counted by its own class, not on a frame clock. The Beta 2 work on special moves covered the frame-clock moves and the buttons, not these, so in the new client these units fight as plain ones. Since then the counted beats are built (`beats` in `client/src/rules/specials-table.json`), and most of those rows now say built or partly; the six left are two towers, an egg, the witch's frog and the like.

Depth: every class was read through. Long stretches that only tween pictures were skimmed. Where a row says the original has a slip (a check the wrong way round, a count that never resets), that is how the code reads; nobody has checked it in the Flash game yet. Item names and ids come from `config/game_config_20120826.json` and `config/patch/unit_patch.json`.
